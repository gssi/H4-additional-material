# H⁴-VALUE — Supplementary Material

Supplementary material for the project **H⁴-VALUE** (*Heritage, Haptic, Human and High-Tech Values in Architectural Living-Use Ecosystems*), submitted to the **PRIN 2026** call (D.D. n. 2298 of 10-04-2026, Italian Ministry of University and Research — MUR), macro-sector **SH** — *Social Sciences & Humanities*.

This repository collects materials supporting the proposal (literature review, data, code, conceptual models) that cannot be fully included within the 20,000-character limit of section C.1 of the application form. ## 1. Literature Review — Digital Twins for Cultural Heritage

Review of the literature on **Digital Twins for Cultural Heritage**, focusing on three dimensions: (i) heritage conservation, (ii) user interaction and usage modes, (iii) Quality of Experience (QoE) and perceptual/socio-economic impact. Papers were selected to cover the four project Challenges (CH1–CH4) and to support the state-of-the-art positioning in section C.1.a of the proposal.

### 1.1 Methodology

A corpus of **51 contributions** (systematic reviews, theoretical frameworks, case studies, empirical studies) was selected, predominantly published between **2019 and 2025** in indexed venues (Q1/Q2 journals in Engineering, Heritage, Tourism Research, Information Systems; IEEE/ACM/Springer proceedings). For each contribution, 18 attributes were extracted and grouped into 4 macro-sections:

| Macro-section | Attributes |
|---|---|
| **A — Bibliographic identification** | Title · Authors · Year · Journal/Venue · Citations · URL |
| **B — Characterisation** | Study type · Digital Twin type · Site context · Key technologies |
| **C — Thematic aspects** | Conservation (+notes) · User interaction (+notes) · QoE/Impact (+notes) |
| **D — Metadata** | Recent (≥2022) · Primary focus |

**Coding of thematic aspects** (columns `Cons`, `Inter`, `QoE` in the table below):

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ | Explicitly addressed, with dedicated discussion |
| ⚠️ | Mentioned or partially addressed |
| ❌ | Not addressed |

### 1.2 Thematic synthesis

Distribution of contributions by thematic aspect:

| Aspect / Focus | ✅ Addressed | ⚠️ Partial | ❌ Not addressed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Heritage conservation | 23 | 13 | 15 |
| User interaction / usage modes | 21 | 14 | 16 |
| QoE / perceptual and socio-economic impact | 18 | 11 | 22 |

Distribution by primary focus of the contribution:

| Primary focus | N° of contributions |
|---|---:|
| Conservation | 20 |
| Interaction | 11 |
| QoE / Interaction | 5 |
| QoE / Socio-economic | 8 |
| QoE | 3 |
| Conservation + Interaction | 2 |
| Conservation + Socio-economic | 1 |
| Conservation (case study without DT integration) | 1 |

> **Main gap identified by the review** — Most contributions address single dimensions (conservation *or* interaction *or* QoE), while only a few explicitly integrate all three. This is the gap that H⁴-VALUE aims to fill through its integrated Heritage–Haptic–Human–High-Tech framework.
